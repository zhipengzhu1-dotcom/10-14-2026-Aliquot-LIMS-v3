# Intake review

A received Sample waits for a decision before its Tests can be assigned. The decision is separate from the Sample's `status` (which stays `Received` and is derived from its Tests): `sample.intake` is `Pending review`, `On hold` or `Accepted`. Receipt, from the direct form or from a client Submission, always starts at `Pending review`. This map covers what exists so far: the state, the accept, hold, release and reject actions, the assign/claim refusal, and the Receiving & Review screen with its Queue and sidebar badge. Assigning from the screen (below) is in; the detail pane and feasibility come in later tickets of spec #48, and this map grows with them. Code: rules `SAMPLE_RULES.accept`, `SAMPLE_RULES.hold`, `SAMPLE_RULES.release`, `SAMPLE_RULES.reject` and the Test gate in `TEST_RULES.assign` / `TEST_RULES.claim`, Queues `SAMPLE_QUEUES.intake` (rule `accept`) and `SAMPLE_QUEUES.ready` (rule `assign`) in `server/workflow.js`, routes, `acceptSample`, `acceptSamples`, `holdSample`, `releaseSample`, `rejectSample` and `tellClient` in `server/routes/intake.js`, the screen in `public/js/views/intake.js` (hold prompt in `public/js/views/intake-hold.js`), state names `INTAKE_PENDING`, `INTAKE_HOLD`, `INTAKE_ACCEPTED` in `server/lookups.js`. Permission `samples.review`: manager and scientist, the same roles as `tests.assign`; analysts, QA, business and the administrator are refused with 403.

## Sub-features

- Accept: `POST /api/samples/<id>/accept`, no body. Allowed while `intake` is `Pending review` or `On hold` and the Sample is open. Refused: 403 without `samples.review` (checked first), 400 "This sample is already accepted", 400 when the Sample is cancelled or otherwise closed, 404 for an unknown id. The Sample's `can.accept` says whether the signed-in person is offered it. A Sample that arrived in a client Submission also gets one staff-authored message in that Submission's portal thread, through `tellClient` (`kind` `message`, author the person who accepted it): "S-… has been accepted and will be tested. Expected completion <d Mon yyyy>." Bulk accept applies the same call to each Sample it accepts, so a Submission of three Samples gets three messages and a refused Sample none; a directly received Sample posts nothing.
- Hold: `POST /api/samples/<id>/hold` with `{ "reason": "…" }`. Allowed only while `intake` is `Pending review` and the Sample is open; moves it to `On hold`. Refused: 403 without `samples.review` (checked first, even with no reason), 400 "This sample is already on hold", 400 when it is Accepted or closed, 400 `REASON_REQUIRED` for a missing or blank reason, 404 unknown id. A Sample that arrived in a client Submission also gets one staff-authored message in that Submission's portal thread (`kind` `message`, author the person who held it, body "Question about S-…, which is on hold until we hear from you: <reason>"), through `tellClient(ctx, sample, body)`; a directly received Sample posts nothing and makes no thread. `portal.respond` is not needed.
- Release: `POST /api/samples/<id>/release`, no body. Allowed only while `On hold`; moves the Sample back to `Pending review` and posts nothing. 403 without `samples.review`, 400 "This sample is not on hold". Accept is also allowed straight from `On hold`. Tests of a held Sample stay unassignable (the assign gate below).
- The assign gate: while `intake` is not `Accepted`, `POST /api/tests/assign` and `POST /api/tests/<id>/claim` are refused with 400 "S-… has not been accepted at intake review yet — accept it before its tests are assigned or picked up". `can.assign` on the Sample and its Tests, `can.claim` on its Tests and `assignable` on the Sample are all empty or false until it is accepted.
- Receiving & Review: `GET /api/intake?tab=pending|hold|ready` (also `client_id`, `project_id`, `priority`, `overdue=1`, `q`) returns `{ tab, counts: {pending, hold, ready}, groups: [{ submission: {id, code, status, received_at} | null, samples: [...] }] }`. Each Sample is the usual Sample row plus `can` (every `SAMPLE_RULES` flag) and `locks` (the refusal message of each action the screen offers, `null` when allowed). Groups are the Submissions the Samples arrived in, in Queue order (due date, then priority); Samples received directly form one group with `submission` null. Pending review and On hold read `SAMPLE_QUEUES.intake`; Ready to assign reads `SAMPLE_QUEUES.ready` (accepted Samples with Tests left to assign). A person without `samples.review` (or, for Ready to assign, `tests.assign`) gets `groups: []` and zero counts. An unknown tab is 400.
- Bulk accept: `POST /api/samples/accept` with `{ "sample_ids": [..] }` applies the accept rule to each Sample on its own and answers 200 with `{ results: [{ id, code, ok, status?, error_code?, error? }], accepted, refused }`, in the order asked. A refusal (403 without the permission, 400 already accepted or cancelled, 404 unknown id) is that Sample's outcome and blocks none of the others. An empty list is 400.
- Reject: `POST /api/samples/<id>/reject` with `{ "reason": "..." }` (rule `SAMPLE_RULES.reject`, stricter than `cancel`: it needs `samples.review`, not `tests.cancel`). Refused: 403 without `samples.review`, 400 `REASON_REQUIRED` without a reason, 400 once any Test is past Pending ("Testing has started on this sample"), 400 once the Sample is accepted, 400 when an Investigation is open on it or its Tests, 400 when the Sample is already cancelled or otherwise closed. Allowed means the Sample's `can.reject`. It cancels the Sample and its open Tests (`status` Cancelled; `intake` is left as it was) and posts "S-… has been rejected and will not be tested. Reason: …" as the signed-in person in the Submission's portal thread (no message for a directly received Sample); audit: a STATUS entry "Sample cancelled" with the reason and a CREATE entry on `intake_decisions` ("Sample rejected at intake review", decision `Rejected`).
- What the client sees (Client portal, `public/portal/portal.js`): `GET /api/portal/samples`, `/api/portal/samples/<id>` and a Submission's `received_samples` carry `intake` (`Pending review`, `On hold`, `Accepted`; `null` once the Sample is Cancelled, so a rejected Sample reads from its `status`) and no staff id or reason. The portal words it "Awaiting review", "On hold" and "Accepted" (pill from `samplePill`, `SAMPLE_LABEL`) only while `status` is `Received`; later the status words (Testing, In review, ...) take over. A Sample's page shows a line `[data-intake-note]` under Progress (on hold it links to the Submission). Another client's Sample is 404 on every one of those routes. Test: `test/intake-portal.test.js`.
- Assign from the screen, per Test: `POST /api/intake/assign` with `{ sample_ids?: [..], test_ids?: [..], analyst_id, due_date? }` (`assignEach` in `server/routes/intake-assign.js`) applies `TEST_RULES.assign` (permission, intake gate) and the qualification refusal to each Test on its own, so one refusal blocks none of the others. A Sample id stands for every Test on it still unassigned. It answers 200 with `{ results: [{ test_id, code, sample_id, sample_code, method_code, ok, status?, error_code?, error? }], assigned, refused }`; a Test of a Sample not Accepted, an unqualified analyst ("… is not qualified on ATM-…"), no permission (403) and an unknown id (404) are outcomes. Nothing selected, or an analyst who is missing, inactive or cannot perform tests, is 400 for the whole request. `POST /api/tests/assign` is unchanged and still aborts its whole batch on the first refusal.
- Assign dialog data: `GET /api/intake/assign-options?sample_ids=1,2` (needs `tests.assign`, else 403) returns `{ tests: [{ id, code, sample_id, sample_code, method_code, method_title, why }], methods: [{ code, title }], analysts: [{ id, full_name, title, role, open, qualified: { "<method code>": bool } }] }`. `why` is the assign refusal for that Test (null when it can be assigned); `open` is the analyst's Pending and In Progress Tests.
- Sidebar badge: `GET /api/nav` has `intake`, the length of `SAMPLE_QUEUES.intake` for the person, shown on the sidebar item "Receiving & Review" (`a[data-nav=intake]`, `[data-badge=intake]`), visible only with `samples.review`.
- Tests on a Sample still waiting for review are not "unassigned work": `GET /api/tests?unassigned=1` (the Worklist Unassigned view), the Worklist's open and overdue lists (`scope=open`, `overdue=1`), the Dashboard `kpis.unassigned` and the Workload `unassigned` count leave them out until the Sample is accepted.
- Each decision is audited twice: a STATUS entry on `samples` (`intake` Pending review → Accepted, summary "Sample accepted at intake review", with the person and time; hold is "Sample put on hold at intake review" with the reason in `reason`, release "Sample released from hold at intake review") and a CREATE entry on `intake_decisions` (the decision record: `decision` Accepted, Held or Released, `reason`, `decided_by`, `decided_at`).
- Samples that existed before the upgrade are `Accepted`. The demo lab is Accepted except its newest arrivals: the two Acme release Samples of the last few hours and the two Samples of the received Acme Submission, which wait Pending review.

- Feasibility indicators (code `server/intake-feasibility.js`, test `test/intake-feasibility.test.js`): `GET /api/intake/samples/<id>/review` (`samples.review`: 403 without it, checked before the Sample lookup; 404 unknown Sample; 401 signed out) returns `{ tests: [{ id, name, method_code, method_version, indicators: { method_usable: {ok, reason}, qualified: {count, ok, reason}, due_date: {ok, reason} } }] }`, one per Test not Cancelled. Method usable = an Effective Method that also has result parameters defined (`methodProblem`, the check requesting a Test applies, stays looser and still accepts In Development and In Validation); any other status is not usable and the reason names it, e.g. "ATM-0010 v1 is in validation, not yet in effect". Qualified = active users in a role holding `tests.perform` with a current qualification (not revoked, not expired) on the Method code. Due date: ok when the Sample's `due_date` (or, with none, the date its priority tier gives from receipt) is on or after the earliest finish, i.e. today plus the Method's `tat_days` in working days; the reason names the tier's working days (`tierDays` in `server/lookups.js`) beside the Method's, so "Same day" is never met by a Method that takes days.

- Accepted with changes (code `server/intake-changes.js`, test `test/intake-changes.test.js`): `GET /api/tests/<id>` carries `intake_changes` { accepted_with_changes, accepted_at, accepted_by_name, edits: [{ field, label, from, to, by, at, reason }], added_tests, cancelled_tests }, derived from the audit trail between the Sample's receipt and its Accepted decision (edits after accepting, custody moves and the Tests requested at receipt do not count; a Sample not yet accepted reports false). The Test page shows `[data-intake-changes]` with one `[data-change=edit|added|cancelled]` line each.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Sign in as priya.raman and click "Receiving & Review" in the sidebar (Laboratory group, under Samples), or open `/samples/review` (`?tab=hold`, `?tab=ready`, filters `client`, `project`, `priority`, `overdue=1`, `q` live in the URL). The page is `public/js/views/intake.js`:

- Tabs `.segmented a` "Pending review", "On hold", "Ready to assign", each with its count; selects `[data-filter-client]`, `[data-filter-project]`, `[data-filter-priority]`, checkbox `[data-filter-overdue]`, search `[data-filter]` (debounced, reloads the list in place).
- One card per group, `[data-group="<submission id>|direct"]`, with a row `[data-row=<sample id>]` per Sample and a checkbox `[data-pick]` (`value` is the Sample id; the header `[data-all]` ticks the group).
- Per-Sample action `button[data-act=accept][data-id=<id>][data-code=S-…]`: enabled only when `can.accept`; when disabled it carries the refusal as `title` and as a visible `[data-why=accept]` line beside it (on the Ready to assign tab: "This sample is already accepted").
- Per-Sample `button[data-act=hold]` (Pending review tab only) opens a modal "Put S-… on hold" with `.modal textarea[name=reason]` and `.modal [data-submit]`; a blank reason shows `.modal .form-error`. The note names the Submission thread when the Sample has one and says no message is sent when it has none. On the On hold tab the row offers `button[data-act=release]` beside Accept. The toast reads "S-… put on hold" / "S-… released". The sidebar badge counts On hold Samples too (they still wait for a decision).
- Per-Sample action `button[data-act=reject]` (same row, enabled by `can.reject`, disabled with `[data-why=reject]` otherwise, e.g. on Ready to assign): opens the reason prompt (`.modal textarea[name=reason]`, `.modal-foot button[data-submit]`; blank stays open with "Reason is required"), then toast "S-… rejected", the Sample leaves the tab and the badge drops.
- Bulk: `[data-bulk=accept]` ("Accept selected (n)", disabled until something is ticked) opens a confirm dialog (`.modal [data-ok]`), then a card `[data-outcomes]` lists every Sample as Accepted or Refused with the server's reason; `[data-select-all]` ticks every Sample shown; `[data-dismiss]` closes the outcome card.
- Assign: `button[data-act=assign][data-id=<id>]` per Sample, enabled only when `can.assign` (accepted, with a Test still unassigned); disabled it shows `locks.assign` ("This sample has not been accepted at intake review yet — …") in a `[data-why=assign]` line. `[data-bulk=assign]` ("Assign selected (n)") takes the ticked Samples. Both open `public/js/views/intake-assign.js`: `.modal [data-analyst=<user id>]` rows, each with a radio `input[name=analyst_id]`, a green or red badge `[data-qualified=<method code>][data-ok=1|0]` per Method and an `[data-open]` count; optional due date; `.modal [data-submit]`. Then a toast ("2 tests assigned, 1 refused") and a `[data-outcomes]` card listing every Test as Assigned or Refused with the reason. A Sample with Tests left (refused ones) stays under Ready to assign.
- A person without the permission (tom.fletcher) has no sidebar item; `/samples/review` shows the empty state "Nothing waiting for review".

- Review pane: the section `[data-section=files]` is the Sample's attachments panel (the one on every record page, upload included); the Client portal keeps no files with a Submission, so there are none from the client. `button[data-review=<sample id>]` on each row opens a modal (`public/js/views/intake-review.js`, `openReview(sampleId)`) whose section `[data-section=feasibility]` (`public/js/views/intake-feasibility.js`) has a row `[data-test=<test id>]` per Test, with cells `[data-indicator=method_usable|qualified|due_date]` (`data-ok` true/false), each a badge with the reason as its `title` and written under it. A row with a not-usable Method or no qualified analyst carries `[data-flag]` "Needs attention" (reason in its `title`) and `data-flagged=true`. To see it, create through the API a Method nobody is qualified on (`POST /api/methods` with `status` In Development and `analytes`), make another Effective and retire it (`POST /api/methods/<id>/status` with `password`), then receive a Sample with both (`method_ids`) and open it.

To prove a bulk refusal in the browser, tick two Pending review Samples, then cancel one through the API (`POST /api/samples/<id>/cancel` with a reason) before clicking "Accept selected": the card shows one Accepted and one Refused ("Sample is cancelled and can't be accepted"), and the toast reads "1 sample accepted, 1 refused". Drive the API directly as a person with the permission:

```bash
.claude/skills/verify-lims/scripts/api.mjs verify priya.raman GET '/api/samples?limit=50'      # pick a sample, then read its intake
.claude/skills/verify-lims/scripts/api.mjs verify priya.raman GET '/api/samples/<id>'          # sample.intake, can.accept, assignable
.claude/skills/verify-lims/scripts/api.mjs verify priya.raman POST '/api/samples/<id>/accept'
```

End state that proves it:

- **Screen:** after Accept the toast reads "S-… accepted", the Sample leaves Pending review, the tab counts and the sidebar badge drop by one, and the Sample appears under Ready to assign.
- **API:** `GET /api/samples/<id>` shows `sample.intake` `Accepted`, `can.accept` false and `assignable` listing its unassigned Tests.
- **Audit:** `GET /api/history/samples/<id>` has the "Sample accepted at intake review" entry under the accepting person's name.
- **Refusals:** as tom.fletcher the same POST returns 403 (the bulk POST returns 200 with `ok: false, status: 403` for each Sample); a second accept returns 400; assigning a Test of a Pending review Sample returns 400 naming intake review (`api.mjs verify priya.raman POST /api/tests/assign` with `{"test_ids":[<id>],"analyst_id":<id>}`).

## Gotchas

- Seeded Pending review Samples: the demo opens with 4 in the Queue (badge 4 for priya.raman), two of them the received Submission SUB-2026-0001. The Ready to assign tab starts empty because every other seeded Sample already has its Tests assigned; accepting one makes it appear there.
- The sweep in `test/agreement.test.js` builds Pending review Samples with `receiveSample({ pendingReview: true })`; every other helper in `test/` accepts the Sample straight after receipt.

## Review values and corrections (#54)

Code: `GET /api/intake/samples/<id>/review` in `server/routes/intake.js` (needs `samples.review`: 403 without it, 404 for an unknown Sample), computed by `reviewValues` in `server/intake-values.js`; the pane `public/js/views/intake-review.js` (`openReview`) with its values section in `public/js/views/intake-values.js`; `intakeHistory` for the Sample detail.

- Review keys (values half): `sample` {id, code, intake, status}; `submitted` null for a Sample received at the front desk, else `{ submission: {id, code, status, received_at, notes, courier, tracking_no, ship_date}, row }` (the client's stored row for this Sample); `current` (the Sample's values now); `fields` [{ key, label, submitted, current, changed }] (`submitted` null without a Submission; a row inherits the Submission's sample type, turnaround and storage); `condition` {condition, storage, location, received_at, received_by_name, receipt_note}; `details` {notes, submission_notes, sheet: [{label, value}]}; `thread` null or {id, subject, status, messages: [{author_name, body, kind, side: client|lab|system, created_at}]}; `corrections` {can, locks (edit, addTests), reason_required, tests: [{id, code, status, method_code, method_version, method_title, can, locks}]}.
- Corrections use the existing actions: `PUT /api/samples/<id>` (description, batch_no, client_ref, priority, storage; a reason is required once `status` is not Received), `POST /api/samples/<id>/tests` `{method_ids}`, `POST /api/tests/<id>/cancel` `{reason}` (needs `tests.cancel`, manager only: a scientist sees `corrections.tests[].can.cancel` false and the refusal in `locks.cancel`). Each is audited with old and new value (`/api/history/samples/<id>`, `/api/history/tests/<id>`).
- Sample detail: `GET /api/samples/<id>` has `intake` `{ state, decisions: [{ id, decision, reason, decided_by_name, decided_at }] }`, newest first.
- Screen: on Receiving & Review each row has `button[data-review=<sample id>]` which opens the pane (`.modal.intake-review`, `[data-section=values]`). Table `[data-values]` rows `[data-field=<key>][data-changed=true|false]`; `[data-edit-sample]` opens the correction form (`name=batch_no`, `priority`, `reason`...; a refusal shows inline); `[data-add-test]` opens `select[name=method_id]`; each Test row `[data-correct-test=<id>]` has `[data-cancel-test=<id>]` (opens the reason dialog); disabled actions show their refusal in `[data-why]`; `[data-thread]` lists the messages. The Sample page (`/samples/<id>`) has an "Intake review" card with `[data-intake-history]`.
