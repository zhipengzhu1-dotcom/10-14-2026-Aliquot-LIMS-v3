# Client portal

A separate sign-in at `/portal/` where a client's contacts follow their own samples, download issued CoAs, submit samples and message the lab. Each contact only sees their own company's records. Code: `public/portal/portal.js` (hash routes), server `server/routes/portal.js`, staff side `public/js/views/portal-inbox.js`.

## Sub-features

- Sign-in card: form `#signin` with `input[name=email]`, `input[name=password]` and `#signin button[type=submit]` "Sign in". Below it is one demo button per contact: `button[data-demo="customer@example.com"]` (Dr. Laura Chen, Acme), `felix.romero@bluestone-bio.example` (Bluestone) and `hannah.schultz@northwind-tx.example` (Northwind). A first sign-in with a temporary password lands on form `#pw` "Choose your password".
- Samples: `#/samples?tab=open|reported|all|shipments`, detail at `#/samples/<id>`, CoA at `#/coa/<id>`. A submission's detail is `#/submissions/<id>`.
- Submit samples (`#/submit`, "Tell us what is coming") is the submission desk: the conversation with the assistant (`.as-chat-col`) beside the draft it fills (`.as-draft-col`). Below 900 px wide only one shows, switched by `.as-tabs button[data-tab=chat|draft]` ("Conversation" / "Submission" with a count of what still blocks sending in `.as-count`). The desk keeps its conversation and draft while the client moves around the portal and starts fresh after sending.
  - Conversation: `textarea#as-message` with `.js-composer button[type=submit]` "Send message" (Ctrl/Cmd+Enter sends), `button.js-paste` "Attach spreadsheet", and the picker `select.js-pick` ("Opus 5.5 · medium effort"; values `<model>|<effort>` from the `MODELS` x `EFFORTS` table) that picks the model and effort for this conversation. Replies are `.msg.lab.as-reply`; their meta `.js-answered-by` reads "<model that answered> · <effort> effort · <seconds> s", plus "· N tries" after a retry. The model's questions show as `button.as-choice[data-answer]`; a click sends that option as the client's answer. While a turn runs `.as-typing` shows, the draft stays editable but its rows are fixed (`button[data-add]` and `button[data-remove]` disabled, `.js-paste-add` says to wait), Send to lab is blocked with "the assistant is still drafting", and every field the client changes in that time keeps the client's value and "you edited" over the reply; a real call takes 15 to 25 s, so wait with `--timeout 180000` on `!document.querySelector(".as-typing")`. With the assistant off or unreachable the chat column shows `.as-off` ("The assistant is off." / "The assistant could not be reached.") and the draft is the whole form.
  - Draft: "Before it goes" (`.as-ledger`): Checked lists the gate's problems (`li.ask`, with `button[data-fix]` "Use …" when the gate offers a value) and what the checks fixed (`li.fixed`); Guessed lists each distinct guess once (`li.guess`, "needed to send" on storage and ship date) with `button[data-confirm]` "Looks right", which marks it "you edited". Then the form (`.as-form`): every field is `[data-field=<path>]` (`project_id`, `storage`, `samples.0.batch_no`) holding an input or select with id `d-<path with dashes>` (e.g. `#d-samples-0-description`, `#d-courier`) and `data-path`; its tag `.ptag` reads from you, from sheet, guessed (dashed amber), fixed (blue), default, you edited, or needs you (orange). Tests are `label.chip input[data-method]`. Samples show as a table at 560 px of draft width and up (so at 1280 and 1440 wide, with the draft column about 605 px), cards below; the table leads with # and Batch / lot, which stay pinned while the other columns scroll sideways inside `.as-tbl`; `button[data-add]` "Add a sample", `button[data-remove]`. "Add from a spreadsheet" (`button.js-paste`) opens `.as-paste`: the same reader as before (`textarea.js-paste-text`, `input.js-file`, `.js-map`, `.js-preview`, `.js-paste-add` "Add N samples"), its rows joining the draft marked from sheet.
  - Every edit runs `POST /api/portal/submissions/check`. The send bar `.as-send` says what still blocks: every gate problem, then storage and ship date while guessed ("confirm storage (…)"); other guesses are only counted. `button.js-send` "Send to lab" stays disabled until nothing blocks, sends once through `POST /api/portal/submissions`, and goes to the confirmation below.
  - The server checks every submission through one gate, `SUBMISSION_GATE` in `server/submission-gate.js`. `POST /api/portal/submissions/check` runs it on a body without saving and returns `{ ok, problems: [{ path, rule, message, fix? }] }`, every problem at its path (`storage`, `samples.<index>.stage`, `method_ids`); `fix.value` is set when the gate knows the value meant (an option differing only in case, spaces or dashes; the tests list without the ones the client cannot order). `POST /api/portal/submissions` refuses with the first problem's message.
- Submission assistant (server side). Off by default. An administrator turns it on in the Staff app at `/settings`, card "Submission assistant": `button[data-act=assistant-on]` "Turn on" or `button[data-act=assistant-off]` "Turn off", and the selects `[name=assistant_model]` and `[name=assistant_effort]` for the default (Opus 5.5, medium). The allowed choices are one table, `MODELS` x `EFFORTS` in `server/assistant.js` (Opus, Sonnet and Haiku 5.5 at low, medium or high); anything else is refused with 400 "Model must be one of: ..." or "Effort must be one of: ...". Turning on runs `claude auth status` on the server's computer and is refused with 400 "No Claude account is signed in on this computer. On the computer that runs Aliquot, run: claude auth login" when none is; otherwise the audit gets an `UPDATE` on `settings`, "Submission assistant turned on with the Claude account <email>, <plan> plan (Opus 5.5, medium effort by default)", under the admin. `GET /api/settings/assistant` (admin only) returns `{ setting, account, problem }`; the `assistant_*` keys are left out of `GET /api/settings` and ignored by `PUT /api/settings`. The portal reads `GET /api/portal/assistant` (`{ enabled, model, model_label: "Opus 5.5", effort: "medium", choices }`) and drafts with `POST /api/portal/assistant` `{ message, draft, history, model?, effort? }`, which answers `{ reply, draft (body + provenance + questions), gate, attempts, unresolved, model, model_label, effort, ms }` and saves nothing; 503 `ASSISTANT_UNAVAILABLE` when it is off or Claude cannot be reached. `server/claude.js` is the only module that runs the `claude` command (`ALIQUOT_CLAUDE_BIN` overrides the binary; the tests point it at their stand-in, test/fake-claude.mjs). It runs the command in an empty folder of its own under the system temp folder, at most two at once, and stops one after 120 s (`ALIQUOT_CLAUDE_TIMEOUT_MS` shortens that), killing it 2 s later if it ignores the stop, with 503 "Claude did not answer within N seconds.". A real call takes several seconds, so wait on the response, not a fixed time.
- Confirmation: after sending, the portal goes to `#/submissions/<id>?sent=1`. While the submission is open, that page shows `.label-card` with the number in `.label-card .big` and the buttons `.js-copy` "Copy number" and `.js-print` "Print"; a "What happens next" card with the status pill and `ol.timeline`; and the links "Your shipments" and "Send another". Without `?sent=1`, or once the submission is closed, the same route shows a normal page head with the code as `h1` and a "Conversation" link.
- Timeline (`SUBMISSION_STEPS`): "Sent to the lab" → "Lab is expecting it" → "Box received". It ends at Received; after that each sample has its own progress, linked under "Logged as S-…". A Declined or Withdrawn submission stops after its last reached step, with an extra `li.stop` row. The `SUBMISSION` table gives each status its pill: Submitted "Sent to lab", Acknowledged "Expected", Received "Received", Declined "Declined", Withdrawn "Withdrawn". Look for "Expected" on screen and `Acknowledged` in the API.
- Withdraw a submission: while `can.withdraw` is true (`GET /api/portal/submissions/<id>` → `can`; true while Submitted), the status card shows "Sent by mistake? Withdraw SUB-…. Available until the lab confirms.", where "Withdraw SUB-…" is the link-button `.js-withdraw`. Toast "Submission withdrawn"; the pill becomes Withdrawn, the timeline ends with "You withdrew this shipment…", and the line goes.
- Requests for method development, validation, transfer or other work: `#/requests`, new at `#/requests/new` (form `#request`, "Send request"). The client's request detail is read-only. The lab moves a request only forward: Submitted → Under review → Proposal sent → Accepted (a proposal can also go straight from Submitted), or Declined while open; Accept only from Proposal sent, and a response that keeps the status is a reply.
- Messages:
  - `#/messages` lists threads.
  - "New" (`.threads-card a[href="#/messages/new"]`; the bare selector matches more than one link) opens form `#newthread` with `input[name=subject]`, `select[name=sample_id]` and `textarea[name=body]`, then "Send".
  - Reply with form `#reply` and its `textarea[name=body]`. Cmd/Ctrl+Enter sends.
- Account (`#/account`, which also has its own `button.js-out` "Sign out") and sign-out: `button.avatar.js-me`, then `button.js-signout`.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open `<base>/portal/` and sign in as a client contact.
- Navigate with the top nav: `.nav a[data-nav=overview|samples|submit|requests|messages]`. Below desktop width `.nav` is hidden; use `nav.tabbar a[data-nav=…]` instead.
- Staff see the other side under **Client portal** in the sidebar (`/portal-inbox`). It opens on Messages. Submissions are at `/portal-inbox?tab=submissions` (open ones by default), and a submission's detail is at `/portal-inbox/submissions/<id>`, with Acknowledge, Decline and "Receive samples" (`button[data-act=receive]`) offered from its `can` flags. Acknowledge and Decline need `portal.respond` (manager, QA, scientist, business); Receive needs `samples.receive` (admin, manager, scientist, analyst). On an open submission priya.raman sees all three, tom.fletcher only Receive, and grace.holloway and daniel.okafor get `can.receive: false`, so no "Receive samples". Requests are at `/portal-inbox?tab=requests` and `/portal-inbox/requests/<id>`.

## Driving it with agent-browser

Run the viewport and reduced-motion setup from SKILL.md first. Without it the splash covers the nav in the new tab and the drive fails.

```bash
agent-browser open "$B/portal/"
agent-browser wait --fn '!document.getElementById("splash")'   # the splash can cover the demo buttons after they render
agent-browser wait '[data-demo="customer@example.com"]'
agent-browser scrollintoview '[data-demo="customer@example.com"]'
agent-browser click '[data-demo="customer@example.com"]'
agent-browser wait 'header.top nav.nav a[data-nav]'
agent-browser tab new "$B/portal/#/"                           # clicks stop landing in the sign-in tab (see Gotchas)
agent-browser wait --fn '!document.getElementById("splash")'
agent-browser wait 'header.top nav.nav a[data-nav]'
agent-browser click '.nav a[data-nav=submit]'
agent-browser wait '#d-samples-0-description'
agent-browser fill '#d-samples-0-description' 'Portal verification sample'
agent-browser wait --fn '!document.querySelector("button.js-send").disabled'
agent-browser click 'button.js-send'                           # "Send to lab"
agent-browser wait '.label-card .big'                          # "SUB-YYYY-NNNN"
agent-browser eval 'location.hash'                             # "#/submissions/<id>?sent=1"
```

End state that proves it:

- **Screen:** `#/submissions/<id>?sent=1` with the code in `.label-card .big`, the pill "Sent to lab", "Sent to the lab" as the current timeline step, the Withdraw line, and the links "Your shipments" and "Send another".
- **API:** as a staff user, `GET /api/portal-admin/submissions` lists the new code with status `Submitted`, and `GET /api/portal-admin/threads` has an automatic thread "Sample submission SUB-…".
- **Messages:** after sending, the new `.msg.client .bubble` appears in `#msgs`. The staff badge is `[data-badge=portal]` in the sidebar, the same number as `GET /api/nav` → `portal`. It counts Submitted submissions, Submitted method requests, and threads with unread client messages. A new submission without notes adds 1, because its automatic thread holds only a system event; with notes it adds 2, because the notes are posted as a client message. A new client thread adds 1. Withdrawing takes the submission's 1 off again.
- **Isolation:** a contact from another company (e.g. `felix.romero@bluestone-bio.example`) must not see Acme's records. Under Felix's portal session:
  - GET `/api/portal/samples/<acme id>`, `/samples/<id>/coa`, `/threads/<id>`, `/submissions/<id>` and `/requests/<id>` return 404, and so do POST `/threads/<id>/messages` and POST `/submissions/<id>/withdraw`.
  - His lists hold only Bluestone's rows, and some are empty in the seed: `/api/portal/samples` defaults to open samples and returns `[]`, while `?status=all` returns his 3 samples; `/api/portal/submissions` may be empty. Check that no list contains Acme codes or subjects.
- **Withdraw:** the API then returns `status: "Withdrawn"` and `can.withdraw: false`; the staff list `GET /api/portal-admin/submissions?status=Withdrawn` holds it and the default open list does not; the audit has `STATUS` on `portal_submissions` "Withdrawn by the client". A second withdraw returns 400 ("This submission is withdrawn — contact the laboratory to change it").

## Gotchas

- The portal uses its own cookie and session, separate from the staff app's. Signing in to one does not sign in to the other.
- The nav links render twice (desktop `.nav` and mobile `nav.tabbar`). Scope selectors to `.nav` on desktop and to `nav.tabbar` at phone width.
- On desktop the sticky `.send-bar` can cover buttons in the draft, and agent-browser refuses the click. Scroll the button into view first.
- The send bar re-renders after every check. Wait on the `disabled` state of `button.js-send`, not a fixed time.
- "Withdraw" on a submission (`.js-withdraw`) calls native `confirm()`, which blocks automation. Before clicking, run `agent-browser eval 'window.confirm=()=>true'`.
- Portal toasts are a single `.toast` element, not the Staff app's `.toasts`: `agent-browser eval 'document.querySelector(".toast")?.innerText'`.
- The portal shows a splash and an `.arrive` animation, plus skeleton loaders after about 160 ms. Wait for content selectors, not fixed times.
- `scripts/api.mjs` signs in through the staff login only. For portal API reads, sign in yourself and keep the jar in the run folder so cleanup removes it:
  ```bash
  curl -s -c .verify/runs/verify/portal-acme.jar -H 'X-Requested-With: aliquot' -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
    -d '{"email":"customer@example.com","password":"demo1234"}' "$B/api/portal/login"
  curl -s -b .verify/runs/verify/portal-acme.jar "$B/api/portal/submissions"
  ```
  Every non-GET needs `X-Requested-With: aliquot`, or the server answers 403. A portal cookie gets 401 on staff endpoints.
- **Input wedge after the demo sign-in** (cause unknown, [issue #11](https://github.com/zhipengzhu1-dotcom/10-14-2026-Aliquot-LIMS-v3/issues/11)). In the tab where the demo sign-in was clicked, agent-browser's clicks stop reaching the page from the first click after sign-in. They still report `✓ Done`, but nothing happens: nav links don't change `location.hash`, "Add another sample" adds no row and test chips don't tick. `fill`, `eval` and `open` keep working, so a filled description is no proof that clicks land. Visiting `#/submit` is not the trigger; the wedge shows up without it. Right after signing in, open a fresh tab with `agent-browser tab new "$B/portal/#/"` and drive everything there; the portal session carries over, and clicks in the new tab keep working through `#/submit` and after it. Check `agent-browser eval 'location.hash'` after each navigation click. The cause is not known.
- **Stale script after a `public/portal/` change (hypothesis).** Once, a reused tab still showed the old double sign-in after the fix while a new tab showed the fix; a cached `portal.js` module is the suspected cause, not proven, and a later review could not reproduce it. Open a new tab with a cache-busting query, such as `agent-browser tab new "$B/portal/?v=$RANDOM#/"`.
